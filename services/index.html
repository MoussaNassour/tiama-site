Frequently asked questions

Where are you based, and do you work with international clients?

I’m based in South Lebanon and work fully online. I design for clients in Lebanon, the Gulf, Europe and the United States, and we stay in touch by WhatsApp, email or video call.

What do I need to get started?

Just a short message about your business and what you need. References, brand names and any ideas you already have are helpful, but I’ll guide you through the rest.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A logo usually takes one to two weeks, a full brand identity a few weeks, and packaging a bit longer. I’ll give you a clear timeline before we start.

Which files will I receive?

Print-ready and web-ready files in the formats you need (such as PDF, PNG, JPG and vector files), organised in folders so they’re easy to use.

Can you design in Arabic as well as English?

Yes. I work in Arabic and English and I enjoy bilingual identities, including Arabic lettering, so your brand looks right in both scripts.

How do we handle payment?

We agree the scope and price together before I begin, and I’ll share the payment options that work best for you. Message me and we’ll sort it out.
